Indian listed companies analysed

ESG Performance vs Supply Chain Risk in Indian Companies (BRSR)

Combined BRSR disclosures with trade and supply-chain data in Power BI. IT firms (Infosys, TCS, Wipro) paired strong ESG performance with low supply-chain exposure, while manufacturing and energy firms (JSW Steel, Tata Steel, Reliance, L&T) showed higher environmental intensity and import dependence.

Key insight: strong ESG performance does not always mean stronger business resilience.
